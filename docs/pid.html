Control in biophysics

The control is calculated as $u(t)=c_{\text{P}}\,\epsilon(t)+c_{\text{I}}\int_{0}^{t}\epsilon(t')\,\dd t'+c_{\text{D}}\,\dot{\epsilon}(t)$, with error $\epsilon(t)\equiv y^{*}-y(t)$ between the set point and the state.

The artificial pancreas: blood glucose is read every 5 minutes and insulin is dosed by a discrete PID controller tuned from an identified SOPTD model. Compare meal and lost-sensitivity scenarios, and see what the integral term buys and costs.
