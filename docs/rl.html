Control in biophysics

Drug-sensitive and resistant tumour cells compete for space: $\dot{S}=r_{S}S(1-N/K)(1-d_{D}D)-d_{S}S$ and $\dot{R}=r_{R}R(1-N/K)-d_{R}R$, with $N=S+R$. Each week the dose $D\in\{0,1\}$ is chosen from $N$ alone, and the tumour progresses once $N\geq(1+\kappa)N(0)$. Continuous therapy always treats, AT50 stops at $N=0.5N(0)$ and restarts at $N(0)$, the agent was trained offline, and your rule uses thresholds of your choice.

Learned policy

Probability that the agent treats in the coming week, given the tumour size and whether it is currently treating (solid) or on holiday (dashed). It treats when this is above one half. Your rule is in blue. The dashed vertical line is the progression size.
