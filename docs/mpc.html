Control in biophysics

Light $u$ drives transcription in the cascade mRNA $R$ → immature GFP $P$ → mature GFP $G$, all in fold changes from the dark steady state: $\dot{R}=d_{r}(1-R)+b_{r}u$, $\dot{P}=(d_{p}+k_{m})(R-P)$, $\dot{G}=d_{p}(P-G)$. Every $\Delta t=10$ min the fluorescence $G$ is read with noise, a particle filter updates its estimates of the state $\bm{y}=[R,P,G]^{T}$ and of the drifting parameters $\bm{w}=[d_{r},d_{p},k_{m},b_{r}]^{T}$, and the controller applies the first step of the plan that minimises $\sum_{k}\big[\tfrac{1}{2}(\bm{y}_{k}-\bm{y}^{*}_{k})^{T}\bm{C}_{y}(\bm{y}_{k}-\bm{y}^{*}_{k})+\tfrac{1}{2}\bm{C}_{u}u_{k}^{2}\big]$ over the prediction horizon under the estimated model. Only $G$ is tracked, so $\bm{C}_{y}$ weights $G$ alone and the target $\bm{y}^{*}_{k}$ sets only its $G$ entry.

Parameter estimates

Particle-filter estimates of the drifting parameters against their true values. Time in min.

Comparison

Errors are G minus the target, over the run. Parameter errors are relative, at the end of the run.
